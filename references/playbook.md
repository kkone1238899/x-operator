# The x-operator playbook

Field notes on growing an X account with an autonomous agent. Six pillars, adapted from real growth playbooks and live operation.

## 1. The profile is the conversion page

The follow decision happens on the profile, not the post. A stranger must get *who you are* and *why follow* in 3 seconds. Fix this before chasing reach — brilliant replies on a confusing profile waste the attention. See the `x-profile` skill.

## 2. One IP, every post ladders up

One consistent through-line. Consistency is what converts a visitor into a follower — they subscribe to *more of a known thing*. Flag and cut off-brand posts, even if they'd perform.

## 3. Borrowed reach beats owned reach (early)

At small follower counts, your posts reach nobody. Replies under big accounts' fresh posts are the top of the funnel — but the reply must be genuinely interesting (earns the click) *and* the profile must convert it. See the `x-engagement` skill. Follow the posters' rhythm: check every 1–2h, newest first, early replies win.

## 4. Ride trends, don't manufacture them

Point content at what people already discuss — model releases, launches, drama. Timely relevance is free exposure. Constraint: it must fit the IP. Ride the wave only if the wave fits who you are.

## 5. Diagnose with the growth equation

```
growth = reach × conversion
```

When stuck, find which variable is broken. Low views → reach problem → borrow more reach (hotter topics, earlier replies, trend-riding). Views but no follows → conversion problem → fix the profile and the follow-worthiness of the content mix.

## 6. Cadence is the engine, quality is the filter

Post consistently, but never ship filler — one bad post costs more in algorithmic trust than one missed slot gains. Two strong posts beat six weak ones.

## What the agent does differently from a human

- **Never sleeps on timing**: sweeps run on the posters' schedule, catching posts minutes after they go live.
- **Never ships on vibes**: every topic is scored, every post passes a critic — the gates replace human taste with process.
- **Compounds**: the review loop writes lessons back into this playbook. The operation gets smarter weekly without anyone touching it.
